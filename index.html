FAQ

Common Questions

Can't find your answer? Reach out and we'll get back to you fast.

How much does tree removal cost?

Cost depends on tree size, condition, and location. We provide a free, itemized estimate after an on-site inspection so there are never any surprises.

Do you offer emergency tree services?

Yes. Our emergency crews respond quickly to storm damage and hazardous trees. Call our emergency line any time, day or night.

Are estimates free?

Always. Every estimate, from a single hedge trim to a full landscape redesign, is completely free with no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We serve the North, South, East, and West Districts and surrounding communities. See our Service Areas section below for details.

How often should lawns be maintained?

Most lawns thrive on weekly mowing during peak growing season, with seasonal feeding and cleanup. We'll recommend a schedule suited to your grass type.
